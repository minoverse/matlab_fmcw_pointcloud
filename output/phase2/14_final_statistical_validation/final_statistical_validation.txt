[Limitations]
Target hits are local CFAR detections within +/-1 range and Doppler bins.
Hit rate can include accidental noise detections near ground truth.
Selection methods restrict the same Dense CFAR decisions to selected CUTs.
Sparse FFT/CFAR are not re-executed each trial; equivalence was tested in Groups 4B-2/4C.
Counts are modeled from selected processing bins; no actual latency or energy measured.
The nominal CFAR alpha and noise-only calibrated alpha are both tested.
Only the specified synthetic two-target geometry and Gaussian noise are evaluated.
Confidence intervals are binomial intervals under independent noise-trial assumptions.
No PNG is generated. Completed scenes are saved to a MAT checkpoint.
