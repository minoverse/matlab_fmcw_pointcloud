[Limitations]
Target hit is evaluated on local CFAR CUTs near ground truth.
Peak grouping and AoA are not part of the Monte Carlo hit metric.
Sparse CFAR target hits use Dense CUT decisions restricted by ROI.
Group 3A validated that ROI CFAR decisions equal Dense at the same CUTs.
FFT and CFAR savings are workload projections, not latency or power.
Thirty trials are exploratory and do not establish a low miss-rate guarantee.
