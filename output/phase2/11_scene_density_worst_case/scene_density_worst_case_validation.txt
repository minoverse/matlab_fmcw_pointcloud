[Limitations]
Only 3 trials are used per target density.
This is a workload stress test, not a final statistical Pd estimate.
Top-K Blocks uses all-or-nothing candidate blocks.
At full candidate density, a large block may be rejected by a small FFT cap.
Matched target retention is relative to Dense CFAR detections.
Synthetic targets do not model realistic clutter or multipath.
FFT caps exclude Range FFT, CFAR, AoA and system overhead.
Execution latency and peak memory are not measured.
