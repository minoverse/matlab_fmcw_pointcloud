[Limitations]
All inputs are noise only. No target signals are present.
Sparse full-grid Pfa includes skipped CUTs as non-detections.
Sparse evaluated-CUT Pfa is NaN if no CUTs were evaluated.
A zero sparse false-alarm count can result from an empty ROI.
Sparse Monte Carlo detections use Dense CFAR restricted to ROI CUTs.
An actual Sparse CFAR regression is checked on one trial.
The CFAR scale assumes independent exponential power samples.
Current 4-RX power summation and windowing can change empirical Pfa.
Pfa is measured, not required to equal its nominal setting.
Workload counters are not CPU latency or power measurements.
